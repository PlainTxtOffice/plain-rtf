# Plain RTF privacy policy — publication draft

<!-- Covers the iOS app only. Add other platforms once they exist. -->

Updated October 2, 2026.

## Documents and recovery

The app reads RTF files you choose and edits their text and formatting on your
device. Unsaved changes and document names are retained in recovery drafts in
local app storage. Recovery drafts may retain embedded content from an imported
document.

## Saving and file providers

Save writes to the chosen file. Export writes a copy to your selected
destination. Files in iCloud Drive, Dropbox or another provider may be uploaded,
synchronized or retained by that provider. The app does not operate its own
document server.

## Removing data

Recovery drafts are removed after changes are saved or you explicitly discard
them. Discarding a draft does not delete an original file. Delete original and
exported documents through Files. Deleting the app removes local drafts;
external documents, provider copies and device backups are separate.

## Developer services

This version contains no developer-run analytics, advertising or account
service. Device backups and services you choose, such as sharing destinations or
file providers, follow their own settings and policies.

## Apple diagnostics

We intend to use Apple-provided crash and performance information, when
available under your Apple diagnostic-sharing choices, only to troubleshoot
problems and improve reliability. App diagnostic logging should not
intentionally include document text, task contents, feed bodies, private
filenames, URLs, tokens or other private content. Apple controls its
system-generated reports; we cannot guarantee those reports contain no sensitive
information.

## Questions

Contact opens our website. We use the name, email address and message you
provide to respond to your request. Service providers may process this
correspondence for us. We retain it as needed to resolve the request or meet
legal obligations. Use the same contact form to request deletion.
